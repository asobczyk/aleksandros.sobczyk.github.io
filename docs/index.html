Computing Systems Lab

Huawei Zurich Research Center

I am currently a Senior Researcher at the Computing Systems Lab of Huawei in Zurich, working on AI accelerators (theory, algorithms, software, architecture). Previously I worked for IBM Zurich Research Lab from 2017 until 2024, most of the time in the team of Teo Laino. Between 2020 and 2024 I did my PhD in collaboration with ETH Zurich, supervised by prof. Mathieu Luisier. Before IBM, I received my Diploma of Engineering and M.Sc. from the Department of Computer Engineering and Informatics at the University of Patras, Greece, supervised by prof. Efstratios Gallopoulos. My PhD thesis is available online at the ETH Research Collection.
